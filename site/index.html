Iron Mike

The piece that started this project: one object, traced through sixty years of paperwork, photographs, and the memory of the people who worked around it. It is the model for how the rest of this archive gets told — not as a database dump, but as stories built on top of one.

How it works

  1. Nothing leaves without paperwork. Material moves in small batches under a signed custody receipt, two to four boxes at a time, photographed at pickup and returned on a fixed date.
  2. Everything lands private. Scans enter the catalog as unreviewed and are visible only to authenticated accounts. The database itself refuses to serve them to the public.
  3. The Unit holds the gate. A Unit reviewer approves, withholds, or redacts each item. Only approved items ever become public — and withheld items never appear in the public index at all.

What never gets published

No protected health information. No personnel or HR records — no evaluations, discipline, medical files, home addresses, or Social Security numbers. These are structural exclusions applied at ingest, not judgment calls made item by item.
