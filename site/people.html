Who worked here

A roster is a list. This is the same information as a shape: every battalion, every station, every era, and the people who stood a watch there. Drag the year and the Unit fills and empties around you.

Real structure, invented people

The battalions and stations are real. Battalions 11, 12, 13, 15, 16 and 17, and the stations under them, are taken from the CAL FIRE Nevada-Yuba-Placer Unit 2026 Strategic Fire Plan — the unit staffing roster in that document. B-14 (Smartsville–Columbia Hill) appears there as a planning area rather than a staffed battalion, so it is not a branch here.

The people are not. Every name, rank, and date is machine-generated from a word list to give the graph something to draw. No real roster, personnel file, or service record was used, and nothing here corresponds to any actual person. On a live archive this view would be built only from information already in the public record — and it would go through the Unit’s review gate first, like everything else.
