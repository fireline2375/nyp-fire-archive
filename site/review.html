The approval gate

This is the only place anything becomes public. Scans arrive here as unreviewed, visible to nobody outside the Unit’s own accounts. A reviewer approves, withholds, or approves with redaction. A few clicks per item, at whatever pace suits the day — nothing expires waiting.

Working demo

The buttons below really do work — they move items in and out of the public catalog for this browser session. Reload to reset. On the live site this page is behind a login and every action is written to an audit trail against a named account — here is what that sign-in would look like.

The flags on these items come from an automated pass that reads every page on ingest. It can only ever flag or escalate — it cannot approve anything, which is why this queue still exists.
