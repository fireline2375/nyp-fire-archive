The release, in plain language

You keep your original. You keep your copyright. You give permission to publish a scan, and you can withdraw that permission later. You tell us how you want to be credited, or whether you would rather not be. If you change your mind, the item comes down.

Asking for something to come down

Anyone — family, retiree, current employee, member of the public — can ask that an item be removed. It comes down first and gets discussed after. No form required and no reason needs to be given.
